# What Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI) changed

Source: consolidated text 02024R1689-20260727 (`data/raw/ai-act-consolidated-20260727-EN.xhtml`),
read from its `▼M1` markers (`32026R1744: REPLACED | INSERTED | DELETED`), cross-checked against the
Omnibus recitals (`data/raw/reg-2026-1744-omnibus-EN.xhtml`). Omnibus adopted 8 July 2026, OJ L 24.7.2026,
in force 27 July 2026. Provision IDs follow docs/CONTRACTS.md §1.

## 1. Changes that affect classification (used by the rule engine)

| Provision | Kind | Change | Engine effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `art:5.1.ba` | inserted | New prohibition: AI systems that generate or manipulate realistic images, video, audio or similar material of an identifiable person's intimate parts or sexually explicit activity without freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous and explicit consent. | Rule `art5.1.ba-bb-intimate-csam` |
| `art:5.1.bb` | inserted | New prohibition: AI systems that generate or manipulate child sexual abuse material (Art. 2(c),(e) Directive 2011/93/EU), unless a national "without right" defence applies. | same rule |
| `art:5.1a` | inserted | Scope of (ba)/(bb): placing on the market is prohibited only if (i) that generation is the intended purpose, or (ii) it is a reasonably foreseeable, reproducible outcome without significant technical modification and the system lacks reasonable and adequate safeguards; use is prohibited only where the deployer uses it for that purpose. | Rule `art5.1a.a.ii-foreseeable-without-safeguards` (providers only) |
| `art:5.1b` | inserted | Manipulation that does not increase exposure of intimate parts or alter the nature of sexual activity is not "manipulation" for (ba). | Cited in the rationale |
| `art:113` (point a) | replaced | (ba), (bb), 5(1a), 5(1b) apply from **2 December 2026** (rest of Chapters I–II from 2 Feb 2025). | Obligation `pro-art5` dated 2026-12-02 when only new points fire |
| `art:3.14` | replaced | "Safety component" redefined: a component fulfils a safety function where its intended purpose is to prevent or mitigate risks to health and safety of persons or property. | Cited by the Annex I rule |
| `art:6.1a`, `art:6.1b` | inserted | AI used solely for non-safety aspects (user assistance, performance optimisation, service efficiency, automation, convenience, quality control) is not a safety component — unless its failure or malfunction would endanger health and safety. | Note `art6.1a-not-safety-component` |
| `art:6.1c` | inserted | A product needing third-party assessment only for non-health/safety risks (e.g. radio spectrum, EMC) does not meet Art. 6(1)(b). | Fact `annex_i_third_party_assessment`; note `art6.1c-no-third-party-assessment` |
| `annex:I.A.1` | deleted | Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC removed from Section A. | — |
| `annex:I.B.21` | inserted | Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 added to Section B. | Machinery now treated as Section B |
| `art:2.2` | replaced | For Annex I Section B high-risk systems only Art. 6(1), Art. 60a and Arts 102–112 apply (57–59 only insofar as integrated in the sectoral law). | Section B: tier high-risk, obligations = sectoral compliance only; Art. 50 overlay suppressed with an assumption |
| `art:2.13` | inserted | For Art. 6(1) systems, Arts 9–15 and 17–25 may be limited where Section A legislation gives equivalent protection; delegated acts by 2 Aug 2027. | Not modelled (no delegated act yet) |

**Not changed:** Art. 6(2)–(8) (the Art. 6(3) derogation, the profiling override and the Art. 6(4)
documentation/registration duty are as in 2024), Annex III (all points), Art. 26, Art. 50(1)–(6),
Art. 51–55, Art. 86.

## 2. Changes that affect obligations and dates

| Provision | Kind | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `art:4` | replaced | AI literacy: providers and deployers must "take measures to support the development of AI literacy" of staff; no duty to guarantee a specific level (was: "ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level"). New paras 2–3: Commission/Member State support, Board recommendations. |
| `art:4a` | inserted | Legal basis for processing special categories of personal data for bias detection and correction (replaces deleted Art. 10(5)); extends to deployers and to non-high-risk systems under conditions. |
| `art:10.1`, `art:10.6` | replaced; `art:10.5` deleted | Data governance cross-refers to Art. 4a(1). |
| `art:11.1` | replaced (2nd subpara) | SMEs, start-ups **and SMCs** may provide Annex IV documentation in a simplified Commission form. |
| `art:17.2`, `art:63.1` | replaced | QMS proportionality now covers SMCs; simplified QMS extended from microenterprises to all SMEs. |
| `art:25.2`, `art:25.4` | replaced | Value chain cooperation duties of initial providers and suppliers restated; FOSS carve-out excludes GPAI models. |
| `art:27.4`, `art:27.5` | replaced | FRIA can cross-reference the GDPR DPIA; AI Office template may be an automated tool. |
| `art:43.3` | replaced | Annex I Section A conformity route clarified; notified bodies under sectoral law must apply for AI Act designation by 28 Jan 2028. |
| `art:50.7` | replaced | Codes of practice on marking/labelling assessed via Art. 56(6); implementing act only as fallback. |
| `art:72.3` | replaced | Post-market monitoring plan template becomes Commission guidance, due 2 Sep 2027. |
| `annex:VIII` (Section B points 7, 9) | deleted | Lighter registration content for Art. 6(3) "not high-risk" systems; the Art. 49(2) registration itself remains. |
| `art:113` (point c) | replaced | High-risk rules (Chapter III, Sections 1–3, except Art. 6(5)) apply from **2 Dec 2027** for Art. 6(2)/Annex III and **2 Aug 2028** for Art. 6(1)/Annex I (was 2 Aug 2026 / 2 Aug 2027). |
| `art:113` (point d) | inserted | Arts 102–110 apply from 27 Jul 2026. |
| `art:111.2` | replaced | Legacy high-risk systems placed on the market before the Chapter III application date are covered only if significantly changed in design afterwards; systems for public authorities must comply by **2 Aug 2030**. (Recital: the grace period follows the type/model, not each unit.) |
| `art:111.4` | inserted | Generative systems placed on the market before 2 Aug 2026 must comply with Art. 50(2) marking by **2 Dec 2026**. |
| `art:75`, `art:75a`–`art:75d` | replaced / inserted | AI Office gets exclusive supervision of AI systems built on a GPAI model by the same provider (with exceptions) and of AI in VLOPs/VLOSEs; investigative powers, commitments, fines. |
| `art:99.1`, `art:99.4.da`, `art:99.6a` | replaced / inserted | Penalties may include warnings and non-monetary measures; fines for breaches of Art. 25(2),(4); lower-of caps extended to SMCs for Art. 99(4)–(5). Art. 99(3) (EUR 35m / 7 %) unchanged. |
| `art:57`, `art:58`, `art:60`, `art:60a` | replaced / inserted | National sandboxes by 2 Aug 2027; EU-level sandbox (Art. 57(3a)); real-world testing extended to Annex I systems (Art. 60a for Section B). |
| Others | replaced / inserted | `art:1.2.g`, `art:2.7`, `art:3.14a`, `art:3.14b` (SME/SMC definitions), `art:28`–`art:30` + `annex:XIV` (notified-body codes), `art:40`, `art:42.3` (CRA presumption for Art. 15 cybersecurity), `art:56.6`, `art:64`, `art:69.2`, `art:70.8`, `art:77`, `art:95.4`, `art:96`, `art:97`. |
